## 3. Method

Condensed from docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md (the authoritative version):

- **Data:** RQ, 4,997 questions (2,332 rhetorical), 3,200/797/1,000 splits — table: `assets/dataset_stats.md`; canonical sha256 in `assets/numbers.json`.
- **Model:** Qwen/Qwen3-4B fp16 @ pinned revision; last-token and mean-token pooled hidden states (37 layers), batched extraction verified identical to the upstream batch-1 reference.
- **Compression:** per-layer PCA-64 (fit on all rows = published protocol, primary; train-fitted variant reported side by side).
- **Probes:** diffMean (unit class-mean difference), logistic (BCE), hinge (SVM) — upstream defaults (SGD lr 0.1, wd 0, batch 256, ≤1000 epochs, patience 20 on validation loss), train split only, test touched once at evaluation.
- **Metrics:** AUROC + Hanley–McNeil 95% CI; direction cosine; Spearman ρ + bootstrap CI; top/bottom-20% Jaccard (frac 0.2); shuffled-label negative control.

## 7. Interpretation limits (mandatory — PROTOCOL §9, keep verbatim)

A linear probe establishes **decodability**, not causal use. The paper itself separates separability (Fig. 1) from alignment (Figs. 2–4) and from causal steering (App. F — excluded here). Our report claims only: (i) linear decodability at reduced scale, (ii) (dis)agreement among probes, (iii) consistency with the paper's qualitative pattern. No "LLMs understand rhetorical questions" phrasing. Cross-model claim ("rhetoric is heterogeneously encoded") is reported as *consistent-with*, not *demonstrated-by*, our 4B single-model experiment.
